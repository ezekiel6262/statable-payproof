# Demo video plan — 2:40 maximum

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“Millions of freelancers earn real income but cannot produce a traditional payslip. Sharing a full statement exposes far more than a landlord or reviewer needs. Statable creates proof of income from verified PayPal payments—without exposing purchases or balances.”

Show the landing-page headline and privacy comparison.

## 0:20–1:20 — Working PayPal flow

1. Open the Statable income workspace and point out the Sandbox indicator.
2. Create a payment request for a named client with amount and service description.
3. Approve it in PayPal Sandbox.
4. Show the automatic return, completed confirmation, and updated payment record.
5. Highlight the PayPal capture ID, AI classification, confidence, and explanation.

## 1:20–2:05 — From payments to proof

1. Show recurring-client detection and the consistency score.
2. Select “Rent a home” and create the proof.
3. Show that the reviewer sees monthly income, consistency, client count, and evidence—but no purchases, balance, or payer contact data.
4. Point out the expiry date and cryptographic signature.

## 2:05–2:30 — Control and impact

1. Open the Proofs library.
2. Copy a proof link, then revoke a different proof.
3. Explain that revoked and expired links fail closed.

## 2:30–2:40 — Close

“Statable gives independent earners paperwork that finally matches how they work: verified by PayPal, explained by AI, and private by default.”

## Recording checklist

- Keep the video under three minutes.
- Use only Sandbox transactions.
- Hide browser bookmarks, emails, passwords, and developer credentials.
- Do not use copyrighted music.
- Upload publicly to YouTube and add the URL to Devpost.
